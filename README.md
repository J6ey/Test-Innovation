# Bejeweled

A simple, single-file Bejeweled-style match-3 game. No build step or dependencies required.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## How to play

- Click a gem, then click an adjacent gem to swap them (or swipe/drag a gem on touch devices).
- Swaps that create a line of 3+ matching gems score points and clear the board.
- Match 4 in a line for a **blast gem**, 5 in a line for a **wildcard**, an L/T/+ shape for a **cross gem**, or a 2x2 block for a **quad gem** — each has its own area-clearing ability.
- Swap two special gems directly into each other to combine their abilities into one bigger combo effect. Swap two wildcards together for the ultimate combo: it clears the entire board.
- Cleared gems are replaced from above, and chain reactions score bonus combo points.
- If no moves are available, the board automatically reshuffles.
- Your best score is saved locally in the browser.

## Controls

- **New Game** — resets the score and starts a fresh board.
- **Shuffle** — randomizes the current board without resetting your score.
